# ThinkBit Privacy Policy

_Last Updated: April 2025_

## Overview

ThinkBit respects your privacy. This app does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data. All data stays on your device. We designed ThinkBit to be fully functional without the need for any backend services, accounts, or analytics.

---

## Information We Collect

We do **not** collect any personal or non-personal information.  
There are:
- No user accounts
- No login or signup
- No background data collection
- No tracking or analytics
- No advertisement identifiers

---

## Data Stored on Your Device

ThinkBit stores:
- Your saved facts and quotes
- Your theme and font preferences
- Offline cached facts (last 7 days)

All this data is stored **locally** on your device and is **never** sent to us or any third party.

---

## One-Time Purchase

ThinkBit is a one-time paid app.  
There are:
- No subscriptions
- No hidden charges

---

## Third-Party Services

ThinkBit does not integrate with or rely on any third-party services for data storage or analytics.

---

## Changes to this Policy

If we make any changes, we will update this file on GitHub along with the new app release.
